# FoundersHub.uz Residency Application - Markivo

## 1. Who is the target customer? (Max 160)
Small business owners in Uzbekistan and Central Asia with no marketing team or digital presence - specifically owner-operators of local restaurants, salons, retail shops who need to build and manage their online presence but lack time, knowledge, and resources.

## 2. What problem are you solving? (Max 280)
Small businesses in emerging markets like Uzbekistan have zero marketing infrastructure - no website, social media, or online presence. Western tools like HubSpot assume you already have a team and presence. Markivo solves this by being an AI-powered marketing OS that builds your entire online presence from scratch, then manages and grows it autonomously in local languages (Uzbek/Russian/English).
