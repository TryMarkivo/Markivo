Deleting your data

Last updated: 10 August 2026

This page explains how to permanently delete your Markivo account and everything associated with it, including any data we received from platforms you connected (Facebook, Instagram, TikTok, Google Business Profile, YouTube, or Telegram).

Option 1 — Delete from your dashboard

  1. Sign in to Markivo and open Settings.
  2. Choose Delete account and confirm.
  3. Your account is erased immediately and you receive a confirmation code.

This is irreversible. It cannot be undone by us or by you.

Option 2 — Remove Markivo from Facebook or Instagram

If you connected a Meta account, you can start the deletion from Meta's side instead:

  1. Go to Settings & Privacy → Settings → Apps and Websites in Facebook or Instagram.
  2. Find Markivo and choose Remove.
  3. Meta notifies us automatically. We erase the account linked to that login and return a confirmation code you can check below.

Option 3 — Email us

Write to support@trymarkivo.com from the email address on your account, with the subject Delete my data. We complete verified requests within 30 days and confirm by reply.

What gets deleted

Everything tied to your account, in full:

  • Your account record — name, email address, and password hash.
  • Your business profiles, including category, description, location, brand tone, slogan, and logo details.
  • All content — posts, drafts, scheduled items, templates, uploaded media files, and your conversations with the Markiv assistant.
  • All platform connections and their access tokens, so we can no longer reach any connected account.
  • Competitor and keyword research, dashboard metric history, and Autopilot settings and activity.
  • Sign-in sessions and refresh tokens.
What we keep, and why. We retain a minimal record of the deletion itself — the confirmation code, its status, and the date — so you can verify the erasure later. It contains no personal data from the deleted account. We also keep billing and tax records where the law requires it; these are held by our payment processor and are not used to contact you.
